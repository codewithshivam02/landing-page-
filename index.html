FAQ

Before you board

Who can apply?

Any student enrolled in a degree or diploma in 2026–27. Professionals can join with a Professional pass.

Do I need a team?

No. Come alone and join a team during formation at 09:00 on Saturday. Teams are 2 to 4 people.

Can I start coding early?

You can plan and design beforehand. Every commit in your repo must be dated after 11:00 IST on Saturday.

What should I bring?

A laptop, charger, student ID and anything you need to sleep comfortably. We provide food, power and Wi-Fi.
